# Project Astraeus — Autonomous VTOL Fixed-Wing UAV for Medical Transport

**Project Astraeus** is an unmanned aerial system (UAS) engineered for long-range medical supply transport in remote mountainous regions, developed in collaboration with the **Municipality of Voio**.

As **Project Manager & Lead Aerodynamics Designer**, I directed the project timeline, coordinated with municipal authorities, and led the mechanical/aerodynamic design (Autodesk Fusion 360) and structural prototyping across two engineering phases:

---

## Repository Structure & Engineering Phases

### 1. Full-Scale Design
📂 **[`01_Full_Scale_Astraeus/`](./01_Full_Scale_Astraeus)**
* **Preliminary Design Report (PPR):** Complete study prepared for the *2026 International UAV Free Mission* competition.
* **Mechanical & Aerodynamic Design:** Full-scale CAD models (`Drone_CAD`), airframe sizing, stability analysis, and structural documentation (`Full_Scale_Mechanical_Report.pdf`).
* **Electrical & Subsystems:** Propulsion/electrical simulations (`Electrical_Simulations`) and complete full-scale Bill of Materials (`Full_Scale_BOM`).

### 2. Sub-Scale Proof of Concept (PoC) Prototype
📂 **[`02_Proof_of_Concept_Astraeus/`](./02_Proof_of_Concept_Astraeus)**
* **Prototyping:** Transitioned from the full-scale theoretical study to a low cost PoC airframe to validate 3D-printed structural housings, wing assembly, and transition mechanisms (`PoC_Mechanical_Design_Report.pdf`).
* **Hardware Integration:** Finalized PoC component selection and equipment Bill of Materials (`PoC_Equipment_BOM`).
